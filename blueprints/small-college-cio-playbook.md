# The Small College CIO: Executive Playbook

**Source Work:** *The Small College CIO: A Practical Guide to IT Leadership in Resource-Constrained Environments* (2026) — by **Joshua David Silva**

Resource-constrained colleges and universities do not need Silicon Valley headcounts to deliver resilient cybersecurity, modern ERP/SIS integration, and high-touch student/faculty experiences. They need **systems-engineered governance, disciplined FinOps, and zero-attrition team leadership**.

## 10-Chapter Architectural Roadmap

1. **Understanding the Small College Landscape** — Enrollment-driven budgets, multi-hat executive reality, and regional accreditation alignment (`SACSCOC`, `ABHES`, `ACAOM`, `THECB`).
2. **Aligning IT with Institutional Priorities** — Converting Board & President goals into a measurable 3-year IT Strategic Plan through shared governance.
3. **Maximizing Limited Resources (Higher-Ed FinOps)** — Auditing shadow SaaS, redundant licenses, and vendor contracts to reclaim **20%–35% of IT spend**, plus unlocking federal/foundation grants (`$1M+` / `$250K+` restriction removal).
4. **Building a High-Performing IT Team** — Retaining talent when you cannot outspend Big Tech via personalized professional development plans, cross-training, and servant leadership (**0% key-staff attrition**).
5. **Navigating Cybersecurity Challenges** — High-ROI protections for **FERPA, GLBA Safeguards Rule, HIPAA, and NIST 800-171**: MFA, endpoint protection, immutable backups, and phishing resilience.
6. **Enhancing the Student Experience** — Mobile-first portals, residence-hall connectivity, frictionless SIS (`Banner`, `Colleague`, `PeopleSoft`, `Workday`) and LMS (`Canvas`) onboarding.
7. **Supporting Faculty & Academic Innovation** — Partnering with Faculty Senates to introduce low-cost AWS, IoT (`Raspberry Pi`), and AI classroom pilots impacting **80,000+ student credit hours**.
8. **Driving Operational Efficiency** — Eliminating spreadsheet silos with self-service **Power BI / Tableau** dashboards and real-time budget telemetry for Deans and CFOs.
9. **Engaging Stakeholders & Shared Governance** — Translating technical risk into President's Cabinet, CFO, and Trustee decisions without jargon.
10. **Planning for the Future** — Pragmatic AI adoption, cloud-native integration, and sustainable institutional digital transformation.

## First 90 Days CIO Diagnostic Checklist

- **Days 1–30 (Listen, Baseline & Protect):** Complete MFA + immutable backup verification; meet Faculty Senate, cabinet Deans, Student Government, and every IT staffer 1-on-1; inventory every SaaS/ERP/vendor renewal within 180 days.
- **Days 31–60 (Stop Bleeding & Quick Wins):** Consolidate duplicate contracts to capture immediate `20%–35%` savings; publish top-10 IT service SLAs and deploy live project intake + budget dashboards for Cabinet visibility.
- **Days 61–90 (Roadmap & Shared Governance):** Ratify a funded 3-year IT Strategic Roadmap anchored to student retention, accreditation readiness, and cyber risk reduction.
